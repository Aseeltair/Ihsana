Why we built this

Generosity is not our problem. Direction is.

Every year the ummah gives hundreds of billions in zakat and sadaqah, yet much of it flows to where the need is most visible, not where it is most solvable. This dashboard exists to close that gap: a clear, honest reading of what each Muslim-majority country most urgently needs, so a family can give with the same rigour it would bring to any serious investment.

01

Solvable

We surface only priorities where money reliably moves the needle: maternal survival, children in school, nutrition, livelihoods. Problems we already know how to fix.

02

Urgent

Countries are ranked by the weight of need today, drawn from a transparent model across health, education and economic indicators, not by headlines or proximity.

03

Where to give

Each country resolves to a short list of clear priorities. Curated, vetted charities matched to each one arrive in Ihsana v2. For now, this shows you exactly where to look.
